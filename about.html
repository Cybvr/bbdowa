Who we are

BBDO West Africa is the Lagos studio of the BBDO network. We work across strategy, creative, production and social, on brands sold across the region.

Do Big Things is not a tagline for us. It is the size of the problem we take on.

What we do

  • Brand strategy and positioning
  • Campaign ideas and creative
  • Social and content
  • Production, broadcast to print
  • Media and reputation planning

How we work

Small teams, short lines, no ceremony. Strategy sits in the room with creative from day one and the work gets pressure tested early.

Every pitch runs on the same checklist so nothing gets lost between the brief and the room.
