The client pod

A typical pod has five to seven people who share the brief and are accountable for the result. One person may cover more than one role on smaller jobs.

Client lead

Keeps the client relationship and makes sure the work answers the commercial brief.

Strategy lead

Brings together the business context, audience research and cultural insight.

Creative lead

Develops the central idea and maintains the creative standard across its different forms.

PR & editorial

Defines the public story and works with journalists, editors and relevant communities.

Creator lead

Finds suitable creators, agrees how they will contribute and manages the partnership.

Media & growth

Plans how the work will reach people, then adjusts media and commerce activity as results come in.

Integrated producer

Turns the idea into a workable schedule and manages cost, rights and delivery.

Shared capability network

Content studio

Makes the copy, design, motion and social content required by each pod.

Intelligence

Handles research, listening, audience analysis and campaign measurement.

Growth

Runs paid media, performance work, retail activity, affiliate programmes and partnerships.

Protection

Supports legal review, compliance, usage rights, accessibility and brand safety.
